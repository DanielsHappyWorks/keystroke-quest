# Changelog

## 1.3.3
- Trophy XP rewards halved (now 5 to 500 XP).

## 1.3.2
- 11 cat trophies: Cat Person, Cat Cafe, A Clowder, Cat Sanctuary, Nine Lives, Pet Pal, Good Human,
  Professional Petter, Purr-fessor, Best Friends, Gotta Pet 'Em All.

## 1.3.1
- Hearts, meow and +1 XP now happen together: only when a pet counts. A cat can be petted once a second;
  clicks sooner than that do nothing.

## 1.3.0
- Renamed to Keystroke Quest (plugin id `com.keystrokequest`). Saved progress and settings carry over.
- Sitting cats: tail rests on the ground; collars and scarves wrap across the neck instead of angling out.

## 1.2.2
- "Reset XP" is now "Reset Progress": clears level, XP, cats, trophies, stats and quests. Settings are kept.

## 1.2.1
- Reset XP also removes your cats, so levelling up again earns them again.
- The "Hello, World" trophy pays 10 XP.

## 1.2.0
- A new, unique virtual cat for every level. Cats walk along IDE panels, jump between them, groom and nap.
- Click a cat to pet it: hearts and +1 XP (once per cat every 3 seconds).
- Cats can be turned off or limited (Settings, or the tool window) for performance.

## 1.1.0
- Trophies (achievements) pay 100-1000 XP on unlock, more than any daily quest.
- Daily quests roll over at midnight without restarting the IDE.

## 1.0.0
- Initial release: blips, booms, screen shake, caret trail, fireworks, synthesized sound.
- XP, levels and titles, combo streak, daily quests, achievements, daily streak.
- Themes and sound packs: Neon, Retro 8-bit, Minimal, Sad.
- Reacts to runs, commits, debugger pauses, refactorings and warning-free files.
- Stats card export, break reminders, reduced-effects mode.
